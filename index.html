What v3.0 is testing

Crew Book connection: the assignment picker now uses the same certified GO 202 Rev. 7 + Paper Rev. 8 passenger schedule data already used by Engineer Pay Log. Regular passenger assignments and passenger relief crews are available; Extra Crew-only jobs are intentionally omitted from the picker.

Relief crews: selecting a relief crew resolves the covered assignment for the selected day before routing. A regular owner relief day does not make the assignment disappear — EPL still routes the job for the relief/extra engineer working it.

Legal timing: every train-to-train transfer must be at least 5 minutes. A normal station report requires arrival at least 1 minute before report. West Side Yard requires Penn 20 minutes before report, Jamaica Storage Yard requires Jamaica 20 minutes before report, and Babylon Yard uses the known 35-minute station-to-yard allowance.

Terminal rules still being filled in: when a yard/employee-location access allowance has not yet been certified, v3.0 shows the train path to the associated station but marks the access rule as pending instead of pretending the result is fully legal.

Going home: every connected job can route from its printed release. Pre-release practical routing needs the final working-train arrival; that movement detail is currently certified only for Job 166 in this sandbox, so other jobs deliberately start at printed release.

Transfer intelligence: direct rides and Jamaica transfers are preferred, Woodside is second, intermediate passenger stations are deprioritized, and employee/operational transfer locations are strongly deprioritized.

Service-day crossover: assignment rules follow the report date, while post-midnight deadheads use the actual next calendar day service.
